Shipping and duties

We ship from Lagos. Orders are packed within 2 working days.

DestinationStandardExpress
Nigeria2 to 4 days1 to 2 days
Rest of Africa7 to 12 days3 to 5 days
UK, Europe, North America7 to 12 days3 to 5 days

Outside Nigeria, import duties and taxes are worked out at checkout and paid up front, so there is nothing to pay on delivery.

Returns

Return clothing and accessories within 30 days in their original condition. Archive pieces and made-to-measure orders cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged.

To start a return, send your order number through the contact form below.
